About Us

Comfort, Care & Quality You Can Trust

Welcome to Cozy Threads

Cozy Threads is a proudly local business dedicated to providing affordable winter essentials and professional laundry services. We are built on the values of quality, comfort, and reliability.

Our mission is simple — to keep you warm during cold seasons and ensure your clothes are always fresh, clean, and well cared for.

❄ Winter Essentials

We provide high-quality winter products designed for everyday use, comfort, and durability.

  • 🧤 Warm Gloves – soft, flexible, and insulating
  • 🧢 Beanies – stylish and warm for cold weather
  • 🧦 No-show Socks – comfortable everyday wear
  • 🧦 Thick Long Socks – extra warmth and durability
  • ☔ Umbrellas – strong and reliable for rainy days

🧺 Laundry Services

We offer professional laundry services designed for convenience and quality care of your garments.

  • Wash Only – clean and fresh laundry service
  • Wash & Fold – neatly washed and folded clothes
  • Wash, Fold & Iron – premium full-care finishing

Every item is handled with care to ensure freshness, cleanliness, and quality finishing every time.

💙 Our Promise

At Cozy Threads, we promise to deliver:

  • ✔ Affordable pricing for everyone
  • ✔ High-quality products and services
  • ✔ Reliable and friendly customer service
  • ✔ Consistent care and attention to detail

Stay warm. Stay fresh. Stay confident — with Cozy Threads.
